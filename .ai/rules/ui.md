# Ui

## The mark is the owner's drawing, a picture: regenerate it with mobile-assets/make-mark.py
Since 2026-11-22 the tripod mark is the owner's coloured drawing (source outside the repo, ~/manteion-logo/logo_bilde2.png), not a one-colour SVG, so skins no longer colour it and `--color-mark`/`text-mark` are gone. `python3 mobile-assets/make-mark.py <drawing.png>` cuts it out of its paper and writes resources/images/mark.webp, mark-dark.webp (ink drawn light; Mark.vue shows it under html[data-mode='dark']), the favicon (only the top of the tripod: the whole one is a smudge at 16px), public/icons/* and the inline logo in public/offline.html. Mark.vue is an inline block of fixed shape (233:480): give it a height and `w-auto`. Its lines are fine, so it is shown tall (56px beside the name, 51px in the 56px phone top bar, where the owner asked for it to reach almost down to the content). On the front page from 640 it stands in the line of text as the M of "Manteion": outer legs on the baseline, the middle leg 12.7% of its height below (negative bottom margin), which relies on an inline block without line boxes having its bottom margin edge as baseline; do not turn that wordmark into a flex row. The same script makes the Android and iOS app icons, the status-bar silhouette (thickened, or its legs are under a pixel) and the splash screens; they reach phones only with a new app release (first with android-v1.7). The Android adaptive icon is a plain white background colour with the picture as foreground, the tripod taking half the layer. On the API 36 emulator the home screen draws a coloured ring around the icon (it did around the old one too, and does around Google's Clock): that is the launcher, not the icon; Settings → App info shows it as it is.
